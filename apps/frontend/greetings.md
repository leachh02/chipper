# Home page greetings

Each time you open Chipper's home page, once you're set up, it greets you
with one of these at random. Add your own!

- One line each, starting with `- `, and at most 60 characters.
- Plain text only: no links or formatting.
- Keep it friendly. Puns very welcome.
